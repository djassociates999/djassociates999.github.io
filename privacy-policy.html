Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  |  DJ Associates – Tax Consultants

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how DJ Associates collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website or services.

1 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our website or engage our services:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, phone number submitted via our contact/callback form
  • Financial information: PAN, income details, and tax documents shared for ITR/GST filing (only when you engage our services)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, browser type, and device info collected automatically via cookies
  • Communication records: WhatsApp or email exchanges for service delivery

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • To provide tax filing, GST compliance, bookkeeping, and advisory services
  • To respond to your queries and schedule consultations
  • To send service reminders (ITR deadlines, GST due dates)
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations under Indian law

3 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With government portals (income tax, GST) as required to deliver services
  • With trusted technology tools used for service delivery (e.g., TallyPrime, filing portals)
  • When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority

4 Cookies & Advertising

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. We may also display advertisements through Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it to serve ads based on your visit to our site
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time

5 Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. All financial documents shared with us are handled with strict confidentiality.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with applicable laws. Tax-related documents may be retained for up to 7 years as required under Indian tax regulations.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details below.

8 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., Income Tax portal, GST portal). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies separately.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
